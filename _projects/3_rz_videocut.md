&nbsp;•&nbsp; **Timeline:** Sep 2021 – Jul 2022

## Overview

Rz VideoCut is an AI-powered video editing app that automatically tracks faces and reframes video around its subjects — turning a manual, frame-by-frame editing task into something the app does on its own. It was launched cross-platform on both the App Store and Google Play, and the underlying framework earned a **granted U.S. patent**.

## Highlights

- **Automated face-tracking and cropping** using [MediaPipe](https://developers.google.com/mediapipe) Face Detection with six facial landmarks and multi-face support.
- **Cross-platform app** built in Dart with custom UX/UI, shipped on the App Store and Google Play.
- **Published research:** co-authored and presented *"An Intelligent Video Editing Automate Framework using AI and Computer Vision"* at the International Conference on Information Technology Convergence and Services (ITCSE 2022).
- **U.S. Patent** granted by the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), July 2022.

## Research context

This project began as a research effort into automating video editing with computer vision. The core idea — detecting and tracking subjects to reframe footage automatically — was formalized in the ITCSE 2022 paper and protected by a U.S. patent.
